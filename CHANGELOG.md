# Lookup

## v0.1.0

The first release.

- A search box on Shift+Space for panels and tabs, settings, key bindings, zones, quests, mounts, pets and toys, spells, macros, bag items, currencies, reputations, Legacy Challenges, slash commands, addon launchers, friends and guild members.
- Spells cast on Enter. Shift+Enter opens the spellbook on the spell's tab.
- Command mode: type `/` to list commands, then press Space to add text after one.
- On/off settings change in place.
- A calculator.
- Lookup remembers your picks and the text you add to commands.
